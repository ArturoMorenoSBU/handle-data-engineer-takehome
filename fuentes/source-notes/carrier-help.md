# Brújula portal help

Source: synthetic carrier help page, version 2026-11-02. Owner: Brújula support.
Carrier: Brújula Seguros (`brujula`).

Receipts may reuse the same numbers in different broker accounts. Amounts are USD installment amounts; `outstanding` means the carrier has not marked that installment paid. A source revision belongs to a receipt, not to the time your pipeline fetched it.

The policy surface and receipt surface have different grains. A policy with several receipts is still one policy. A receipt's missing due date is not permission to use the policy end date.
